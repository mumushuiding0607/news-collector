## 英文提示词（推荐用于 Midjourney / DALL-E / Flux 等）

```
App Icon for "WindGuide AI" (风向标AI)
A minimalist yet premium AI news app icon with a dark charcoal background (#1A1A1A).

Central element: A bold golden (#FFB300) wind vane / compass arrow pointing upper-right, symbolizing direction, speed, and first-hand information. The arrow has a sharp, modern geometric style with subtle AI-style circuit line accents.

Surrounding ring: A thin golden circular frame with compass-style tick marks (24 ticks, every 3rd thicker), representing precision and market intelligence.

Four corner sparkles: Small golden four-pointed stars scattered around the periphery, representing AI intelligence and data points.

Bottom area: Clean sans-serif typography "风向标AI" in golden text, with smaller subtitle "WINDGUIDE AI" in muted gray (#B0B0B0).

Overall mood: Dark tech, premium, professional financial/intellectual tool.
No gradients, flat design with subtle depth.
Style: Vector app icon, clean lines, no clutter.
```

## 中文设计说明（辅助理解）

```
【应用名称】风向标AI (WindGuide AI)
【核心定位】AI驱动的第一时间一手资讯，快人一步决策

【设计元素】
① 风向标/罗盘箭头（主体）— 金色，指向右上方，象征速度和方向
② 外圈刻度环 — 金色罗盘风格，24个刻度，象征精准情报
③ 四角星光点缀 — 金色四角星，象征AI与数据节点
④ 底部文字 — "风向标AI" 金色 + "WINDGUIDE AI" 灰色副标题

【配色】
-背景：深炭黑 #1A1A1A
- 主色：金色 #FFB300
- 辅助：暖灰 #B0B0B0

【风格】暗色科技感、扁平高端、金融/情报工具感

【技术要求】
- 1024×1024 PNG（应用市场强制要求）
- 另生成各尺寸 PNG：48×48 / 72×72 / 96×96 / 144×144 / 192×192
- 透明背景 PNG（用于 Android adaptive icon foreground）
- 视觉需在48px 小尺寸下依然清晰可辨
```

## 给 AI 图像生成模型的完整 Prompt（英文版）

```
App icon design for "WindGuide AI", a premium AI-powered news intelligence platform.
Dark charcoal background (#1A1A1A), flat vector style, ultra-clean lines.

Center: A sharp golden (#FFB300) wind vane / directional compass arrow pointing to upper-right at ~45°, symbolizing speed, direction, and getting information first. Geometric minimalist style.

Outer ring: Thin golden circular frame with24 compass-style tick marks,8 major ticks at N/E/S/W positions, representing precision market intelligence.

Corner accents: 4 small golden four-pointed stars at upper-left, upper-right, lower-left, lower-right corners, representing AI data points and intelligence.

Bottom typography: "风向标AI" in golden (#FFB300) bold sans-serif at bottom center, "WINDGUIDE AI" in muted gray (#B0B0B0) smaller subtitle below it.

Overall feel: Premium dark-tech financial intelligence tool. No gradients, no shadows, pure flat vector. High contrast for small-size readability.
App icon, vector style, clean, minimalist, 1024x1024.
```

## 使用建议

| 工具 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Midjourney** | 使用 ` --v 6 --ar 1:1 --style raw` 参数 |
| **DALL-E 3** | 直接粘贴英文 Prompt |
| **Flux** | 使用英文 Prompt，效果最佳 |
| **Figma / Canva** | 导入后二次编辑，调整细节 |

生成后如需透明背景和水印去除，可用在线工具如 **remove.bg** 处理。
